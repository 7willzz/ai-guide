After deep thinking, DeepSeek provided a detailed investment method:

Making 1 million yuan by trading stocks and buying funds is feasible, but for ordinary people, it requires a combination of reasonable strategy, long-term persistence, risk control, and a certain amount of learning ability.

The following is a step-by-step practical guide, covering the key logic and specific methods:

### Step 1: Clarify Goals and Underlying Logic

1. Time and Compounding:

Assume an annualized return of 10% (the long-term average stock market return), and invest monthly via dollar-cost averaging:

  - If you invest 5,000 yuan per month, it takes about 9 years to reach 1 million yuan (compound interest calculation).
  - If you invest 3,000 yuan per month, it takes about 12 years.
  - Core logic: The longer the time, the stronger the compounding effect. Seeking quick success often leads to losses.

2. Risk and Return Matching:

  - If you want to make 1 million yuan quickly (e.g., within 3 years), you need to take extremely high risks (leverage, speculation), but the probability of failure is very high.
  - Ordinary people are advised to choose a steady path with an "annualized return of 8%–15%" to avoid a large loss of principal.

### Step 2: Choose Tools That Suit You

#### 1. Fund Investing (More Suitable for Beginners)

- Index Fund Dollar-Cost Averaging:

  - Target selection: CSI 300, CSI 500, Nasdaq 100 (e.g., exchange-traded ETFs: 510300, 510500).
  - Strategy: Buy at a fixed time each month, add positions when undervalued, and trim positions when overvalued (refer to historical PE/PB percentiles).
  - Advantages: Low fees, diversified risk, and long-term outperformance of most actively managed funds.

- Screening Actively Managed Funds:

  - Screening criteria:
  - Examples: Zhu Shaoxing's Fullgoal Tianhui, Xie Zhiyu's Xingquan Herun.

1. 1. The fund manager has served for more than 5 years, with an annualized return >15%;
   2. Maximum drawdown <30%;
   3. Size of 1–10 billion yuan (too small is prone to liquidation, too large is hard to adjust positions).

- Sector Funds (Advanced):

  - Choose long-term sectors (consumer, healthcare, technology), but position at low levels and avoid chasing highs.
  - For example: invest via dollar-cost averaging when the CSI Medical Index PE <30, and sell in batches when PE >50.

#### 2. Stock Investing (Requires Time to Learn)

- Value Investing:

  - Stock selection criteria: ROE >15%, gross margin >30%, debt ratio <50%, stable free cash flow.
  - Examples: Long-term bull stocks such as Moutai and China Yangtze Power; hold for the long term and reinvest dividends.

- Growth Stock Investing:

  - Pay attention to industry penetration rates (e.g., enter when NEV penetration <30% and AI penetration <10%).
  - Screen using PEG <1 (P/E ratio / earnings growth rate), e.g., CATL during its high-growth period in 2020.

- Key Principles:

  - Single-stock position ≤20%, sector holding ≤30%;
  - Avoid frequent trading; taxes, fees, and emotional wear and tear will erode returns.

Step 4: Risk Control and Mindset Management

1. Position control:

  - In a bear market (PE percentile <30%), you can add positions up to 90%;
  - In a bull market (PE percentile >70%), reduce positions to below 50%.

1. Stop-loss discipline:

  - Enforce a stop-loss when a single fund/stock falls 20% (unless fundamentals have not deteriorated).
  - When overall account drawdown exceeds 15%, pause buying and review your strategy.

1. Emotional management:

  - Avoid chasing rallies and selling into declines (e.g., chasing liquor stocks in 2021, capitulating on new energy in 2022).
  - Use "contrarian thinking": buy when the market panics (e.g., the pandemic bottom in March 2020).

### Step 5: Acceleration Strategies (Optional)

1. Increase principal:

  - Increase income through your main job or a side hustle; raise your monthly investment from 5,000 to 10,000, shortening the target time by 30%.

1. Enhance returns:

  - Convertible bond IPO subscriptions: about 3,000–5,000 yuan in risk-free returns per year (multiple accounts required).
  - Grid trading: set automated buying and selling for index ETFs within a 5% fluctuation range (requires broker conditional order functionality).

1. Arbitrage opportunities:

  - Hong Kong IPO subscriptions (participate when valuations are low, e.g., biopharma B-shares in 2023);
  - Closed-end fund discount arbitrage (buy when the discount is >8%, and redeem at NAV at maturity).

### Key Reminders

- Avoid common pitfalls:

1. 1. Do not trust "insider information" (e.g., the 2021 Rendong Holdings debacle);
   2. Avoid high leverage (margin financing and securities lending, capital allocation);
   3. Do not buy products you do not understand (Crude Oil Treasure, snowball structured derivatives).

- Learning resources:

  - Books: The Intelligent Investor, A Random Walk Down Wall Street;
  - Tools: Tiantian Fund app (check valuations, smart dollar-cost averaging).

### Summary Path

Ordinary person → invest 5,000 yuan monthly in index funds + allocate 20% to high-quality individual stocks → persist for 10 years (10% annualized) → 1 million yuan
(If annualized return rises to 15%, the time can be shortened to 7 years.)

Ultimately, whether you succeed depends on whether you can delay gratification, maintain discipline, and keep learning. The market rewards patient and rational people, not the "smart" ones who predict short-term fluctuations.
