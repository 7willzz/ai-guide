# DeepSeek Told Me: From 30 to 40, People Generally Have This Much Savings

"Not having 500,000 yuan in savings by 30 means you're a failure?"—this short video with a million likes on social platforms has kept countless middle-aged people awake at night. Behind this collective anxiety is a wealth illusion carefully designed by social media.

DeepSeek data shows: among the 30–40 age group, 65% carry loans, and only 15% have savings over 500,000 yuan. More cruelly, the median savings of homeowners in first-tier cities is only 150,000–250,000 yuan, while non-homeowners actually have higher savings (about 500,000+ yuan).

Under the heavy pressure of the three mountains—mortgage, child-rearing, and retirement—the per capita debt of the post-90s generation has reached 120,000 yuan, and the post-80s generation has reached as high as 200,000 yuan. Behind these numbers are the real financial difficulties faced by different age groups.

**Savings are like Schrödinger's cat—before you open the account, you never know whether you're a winner or a loser.**

**01**

**Savings Levels from Age 30 to 40**

DeepSeek, combining multiple authoritative surveys and case studies, shows that savings levels among the 30–40 age group are significantly differentiated, ranging from tens of thousands to millions of yuan. Below are the baseline savings ranges:

**National Overall Level**

Median: about 150,000–300,000 yuan

Mean range: 300,000–500,000 yuan in first-tier cities, 100,000–300,000 yuan in second- and third-tier cities

Extreme cases: internet/finance professionals may reach 1 million+ yuan, while self-employed people fluctuate greatly (0–2 million yuan)

**City Differences**

First-tier cities (Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen): Homeowners: savings are generally squeezed by mortgages (about 150,000–250,000 yuan)

Non-homeowners: higher savings rate (about 500,000+ yuan)

Second- and third-tier cities (such as Wuhan and Changsha): median household savings are about 200,000–400,000 yuan, but families with children may be below 100,000 yuan

**Key Dividing Line**

Age 35: savings benchmark is about 200,000–500,000 yuan (including wealth management investments)

Age 40: ideal savings are 3x annual salary (if annual salary is 200,000 yuan, you need 600,000 yuan)

The contrast between employees at big internet companies and public-sector employees is highly representative:

● An Alibaba P7 engineer has an annual salary of 600,000 yuan, but under the age-35 crisis is forced to build an "unemployment buffer fund";

● A civil servant in a third-tier city has an annual salary of 120,000 yuan, yet through "housing provident fund covering the mortgage + side gig livestream selling" manages to save 80,000 yuan per year.

Wuhan teacher Chen Min (34) and her husband have an annual income of 250,000 yuan, yet live the life of the "poorest middle class."

In 2019, they bought a school-district home with a total price of 2.8 million yuan. After emptying six wallets to pay the down payment, the monthly mortgage payment of 14,000 yuan accounts for 56% of their income. "For our child's extracurricular classes, we can only choose the cheapest ones; the whole family hasn't traveled outside the province in five years," she said with a bitter smile, showing her account: a savings balance of 87,000 yuan, 60,000 yuan of which is reserved for her mother-in-law's heart stent surgery.

On the other side is the counterattack of low-desire youth.

Changsha designer Wang Lei (32) chooses an "anti-formula" way of living: no buying a house, no marriage. His monthly salary is 12,000 yuan, but he forces himself to save 7,000 yuan every month. Through "regular fund investments + new convertible bond subscriptions," he accumulated 530,000 yuan in savings over 5 years. "Colleagues laugh at me for living like an ascetic, but they don't know I've already saved enough in the fund to quit and travel the world."

There is also the story of Shenzhen entrepreneur Li Wei (38), full of drama. In 2022, she went all in on cross-border e-commerce and earned her first pot of gold: 800,000 yuan. In 2023, a logistics blow-up caused a loss of 600,000 yuan. Now she has made a comeback with pet funeral services.

She said, "Savings are like a roller coaster; from millionaire to the edge of debt is only one black swan away."

**Some use savings to buy a sense of security, while others use savings to redeem a ticket to freedom—this is the real consumption stratification among adults.**

**03**

**From Survival Game to Wealth Control**

**So what can be done to break the deadlock and build yourself a wealth system of income while you sleep?**

**1. Build an antifragile financial system**

● Emergency layer: 3–6 months of basic living expenses (starting at 50,000 yuan in first-tier cities)

● Defense layer: critical illness insurance (coverage ≥ 3x annual income) + increasing whole life insurance (locking in 3.5% compound interest)

● Offense layer: regular index fund investments (5%–15% annualized) + ETFs in industries benefiting from policy dividends

**2. The leverage effect of cognitive upgrading**

The experience of Hangzhou stay-at-home mom Lin Yue is highly inspiring: In 2023, with 5,000 yuan in startup capital, she taught "how to use mobile phones for seniors" on Douyin, seized the silver economy dividend, and now monetizes 30,000+ yuan per month. Instead of being anxious about savings, study which needs remain unmet.

**3. Time-space arbitrage strategy**

● Geographic arbitrage: a Beijing programmer remotely takes on Shenzhen projects, using the salary difference to subsidize rent;

● Time arbitrage: use AI tools to "sell one unit of time multiple times," such as simultaneously generating short videos and image-text posts from livestream content.

**"True financial health is not about the savings number, but whether 'income while you sleep' can cover the 'cost of anxiety.'"**

The ultimate truth DeepSeek told me: savings are not the destination, but the ticket to choice.

When we talk about savings, we are essentially discussing control over life:

● 500,000 yuan in savings can let you refuse a 996 job and accept a pay-cut offer;

● 1 million yuan in savings can support an ordinary person's gap year to find a new direction in life;

● 2 million yuan in savings means you can choose an "early retirement experiment."

"The real value of savings is that it gives you the confidence to say 'no' and the capital to say 'yes.'"
